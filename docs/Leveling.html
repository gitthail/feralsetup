Use this as checkpoints

Darkshore → Ashenvale → Wetlands → Duskwood → Wetlands / Ashenvale. This crosses both continents; it is not an Elf-only route. Start near home because you are already there, then follow useful quest density.

An efficient broad route, not a measured speedrun record. Your completed quests, starting location, competition and travel unlocks can change the best next stop. The named quests are a priority backbone, not enough XP by themselves.

Pick up nearby quests sharing the same destination. Move on when the remaining work is scattered, too difficult or heavily contested. Green quests with quick kills can beat yellow quests with long walks.

LEVEL 23–25

Stop / train: Get Menethil’s flight path. At 24 train Rake, Ferocious Bite, Remove Curse and useful Bear/heal upgrades.

This is dwarf/human Alliance territory, not an Elf-only route. Combine excavation and swamp objectives instead of crossing the whole map for one hand-in.

LEVEL 25–27

Duskwood · Darkshire

Stop / train: At 26 train Dash, Abolish Poison and Maul/Healing Touch upgrades. Keep enough money for these.

When to adjust

Redridge leftovers are a nearby alternative. Do not force elite finales or a long courier chain when the nearby kill quests are better.

LEVEL 27–30

Wetlands return → Ashenvale if needed

Stop / train: At 28 train Claw, Rip and useful healing/control upgrades. At 30 train Travel Form and Bash.

When to adjust

Finish a productive local cluster before boarding another boat. If Duskwood still offers easy stacked quests, stay there first. Elite finales are optional with a ready group.

Rules that save more time than a rigid itinerary

  • Batch one geographic loop, then turn in. Hearth to your current hub.
  • Skip isolated errands and elite finales unless the reward or follow-up justifies them.
  • With your friend, favor shared kill objectives. Separate collection drops can slow a duo down.
  • Run a dungeon when the group and quests are ready. Waiting for a speculative run is not speed leveling.
  • Keep easy enemies dying along the route. Repeated corpse runs erase small routing gains.
  • Buy training and needed inventory space before marginal rings or armor. Check bag quest rewards first; your friend already helps with leather.

Aquatic can be learned from its level-16 quest, but strict speed favors combining its travel with the route. Once learned, middle-click selects it while swimming. Refresh macros with /fs update, then /fs save.

Keep Herbalism + Alchemy

Recommendation revised for your preference: batch crafting and occasional auction-house work suit you better than adding a skinning action to every animal kill. Keep your current pair for now; do not restart professions just for theoretical gold.

This is the best fit I can recommend, not a verified maximum-profit pair. A winner requires your region / realm market, recipe access, starting capital and actual sales. Gold per active minute matters more to you than gold per hour spent roaming.

Druid and Herbalism

Movement forms make travel between nodes convenient. That does not make harvesting passive, and it does not make self-gathered herbs free. Travel Form arrives at 30. I have not verified Forever-specific herb interaction while transformed; do not base the decision on Retail flight-form gathering behavior.

A small, repeatable Alchemy routine

  1. Choose two or three recipes you can already make: check mana potions and useful elixirs first.
  2. Compare finished-item sale prices with the full cost of herbs, vials and other reagents. Value your gathered herbs at their alternative sale value.
  3. Make one small trial batch, for example 5–10 items, only when the margin is worthwhile. That is a trial size, not a sales forecast.
  4. List once, then go play. Check sold mail next session. Avoid an endless cancel-and-relist routine.
  5. Repeat what actually sells. If a craft loses money, sell the herbs or wait; do not craft merely because the recipe is available.

Gather only when it is convenient. Buying reasonably priced herbs is compatible with being an Herbalist. At level 15, preserving training money matters more than rushing profession skill.

Take First Aid as a secondary profession. Forever moved ordinary healing-potion crafting there, so Alchemy’s case is mana potions, elixirs and other products—not exclusive access to healing potions. Some gear is bind-on-pickup; purchasing cannot replace every crafting reward. Some Forever explosives are usable without Engineering. Profession details and evidence labels.

Self-crafted gear: count materials, recipes, skill-up costs and time. Crafting can be economical, but your own materials are still valuable. Buy selective tradable upgrades; choose a gear craft for a specific reward you actually want.

Is this batch worth making?

Enter all prices in the same unit (gold or silver). This estimates margin if the entire batch sells; it does not predict demand or include initial profession leveling costs.

Deposits returned on successful sales are not a final expense; include deposits actually lost to failed listings or cancellation. If you gather, include that time in active minutes.
