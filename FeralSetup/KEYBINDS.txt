FERALSETUP 3.5.4 — MAC + WINDOWS

Cmd and Alt are alternatives everywhere Cmd is assigned. On Windows use Alt.
Use one modifier at a time except the explicitly listed targeting combinations.

KEY           CAT                  STEALTHED CAT          BEAR
1             Claw                 Claw                   Maul
2             Rake                 Rake                   Primal Bite
3             Ferocious Bite       Ferocious Bite         Swipe
4             Rip                  Rip                    Demoralizing Roar
R             Shred                Ravage                 Lacerate
Q             Dash                 Pounce                 Bash
E             Cat Charge           Cat Charge*            Bear Charge
Z             Shifting Power       Shifting Power         Enrage
B             Prowl ON             Prowl OFF              Challenging Roar
6             Empty                Empty                  Growl
J             Cower                Cower                  Empty
Cmd/Alt+J     Empty                Empty                  Frenzied Regeneration

* E retains Cat Charge in stealth; the client still enforces its restrictions.
Unlearned spells are unavailable. R and Q do not fall back to a different attack
when the selected stealth opener is unlearned. 1-4 remain ordinary attacks.
No Q/E/B macro changes your form. To Bash from Cat: wheel down, then Q.

MODIFIERS
Alt+Q         Dash in Cat, including while stealthed. No action in Bear/humanoid.
Shift+Q       Focus Pounce in stealth / focus Bash in Bear; inactive normal Cat.
Ctrl+Q        Mouseover Pounce in stealth / mouseover Bash in Bear.
Shift+E       Focus charge in your current animal form.
Ctrl+E        Mouseover charge in your current animal form.
Shift/Ctrl+6  Focus/mouseover Growl in Bear.

 target, then self. Cmd OR Alt = self; Ctrl = living friendly mouseover only;
 Shift alone = inactive. These macros leave form before casting.

FORMS AND FOCUS
Wheel up/down       Enter Cat/Bear; no repeated shift when already in that form.
Cmd/Alt+wheel up     Deliberately re-shift Cat, including from Cat.
Cmd/Alt+wheel down   Deliberately re-shift Bear, including from Bear.
Middle-click        Aquatic while swimming; Travel outdoors on land.
H                   Leave form; stop casting and attacking.
Shift+Space         Set focus: living mouseover, otherwise living target.
Cmd/Alt+Shift+Space  Clear focus.

R Soothe Animal; other main slots empty. Fixed keys still work.

MIGRATION
Faerie Fire moved to Shift+R; previous Cmd/Alt+R assignments are removed.
Focus moved from N to Shift+Space; Cmd/Alt+Shift+Space clears it.
Frenzied Regeneration moved from Cmd+R to Cmd/Alt+J.
B remains Challenging Roar in Bear and toggles Prowl only in Cat.
The old N / key-left-of-1 addon assignments are removed if still assigned to
those addon actions. Unrelated replacements on those keys are preserved.
No bar-bug workaround or general spell-rank macro conversion was added.
