Extract. Move one folder. Play.

Download FeralSetup.zip, extract it, and move the extracted FeralSetup folder into your WoW Forever installation’s Interface/AddOns folder. Restart WoW.

Fresh install or upgrading from before 3.5

Out of combat, humanoid form, main page 1:

/feralsetup setup

Enter Cat, run /feralsetup form. Enter Bear, run it again. Check your bars, then:

/feralsetup save

Setup replaces same-name guide macros and configured bars/bindings. User item slots are preserved. Skip forms not yet learned.

Upgrading from 3.5–3.5.4

Replace files and /reload. Out of combat, humanoid form, main page 1: run /feralsetup form. Then enter Cat and run /feralsetup form again. This places human Q/E and swaps Cat 2/3. Bear and fixed bars need no rebuild.

If upgrading from before 3.5.4, also run /feralsetup update and /feralsetup bindings for earlier macro/binding fixes. After checking the layout:

/feralsetup save

/feralsetup status reports the loaded version.

Mac / Windows: every Cmd shortcut also works with Alt. On café PCs use Alt. Saved snapshots and preferences live separately in WoW’s WTF folder; installing the addon does not transfer those.

Cat, stealth and Bear

1/2: Cat direct damage / Bear single-target attacks. 3/4: Cat bleeds / Bear group tools. Only Q and R select a different spell in stealth.

{{COMBAT_ROWS}}
KeyCatStealthed CatBear
No automatic form changes on Q/E/B. To Bash from Cat: wheel down → Q. B toggles Prowl only in Cat; in Bear it remains Challenging Roar. Alt+Q gives access to Dash while stealthed. Unlearned abilities remain unavailable; stealth Q/R do not fall back to another attack if the opener is unlearned.

E keeps Cat Charge in stealth, but the game enforces its restrictions. The layout does not bypass range, resource, stealth or cooldown requirements. Ordinary attacks on 1–4 can break stealth.

Fixed support keys

{{SUPPORT_ROWS}}
KeyAbilityTargeting
Healing, cures, Mark and Thorns: plain key selects living friendly mouseover → living friendly target → self. Cmd or Alt forces self. Ctrl selects living friendly mouseover only; Shift alone does nothing. These macros leave form to cast. Range and line of sight can still prevent a cast; they do not cause a fallback to another recipient.

Faerie Fire is Shift+R on your current target. Ctrl+Shift+R targets enemy mouseover. There is no focus shortcut for Faerie Fire in this temporary layout. Cmd/Alt+R are not assigned to it.

Defensives, buffs and items

Potions, Healthstone, bandage, food, drink and mount are your own item slots. They are not auto-filled. Cmd/Alt+J is Frenzied Regeneration in Bear; it is inactive in Cat.

Forms, focus and movement

{{FORM_ROWS}}
KeyAction

Deliberate re-shifts spend mana. Cmd/Alt casts the form directly, without cancelling it first. Ordinary wheel presses don’t re-shift you when you are already in that form. Shift/Alt+WASD are not assigned by the addon.

Humanoid’s main page retains 1 Moonfire, 2 Wrath, 3 Starfire, 4 Hurricane and R Soothe Animal. Q = Roots, E = Hibernate: target normally, Shift for focus, Ctrl for enemy mouseover. T Roots and X Hibernate still work in every form. Other main slots are empty.

Every addon command

Layout commands require being out of combat. Nothing auto-restores at login. Do not save a broken layout over the snapshot you want to recover. The legacy leftkey command remains available, but the current layout does not depend on that keyboard key.

The route, inside the game

Private level-up reminders are enabled by default for Alliance Druids from 15 through 30. They show your recommended current area, next area and training notes on milestone levels.

/feralsetup guide
/feralsetup guide off
/feralsetup guide on

guide shows the current route and quest suggestions whenever you need them. off/on changes automatic reminders for this character. Manual lookup still works while reminders are off.

{{ROUTE_ROWS}}
LevelsRecommended area

Flexible checkpoints, not completed-quest tracking. At 30 the addon reports the end of the agreed route; it does not invent a later zone.

Macro reference

The addon detects learned form numbers when you install/update macros. For manual copying, check these values against your character first. Ordinary attack buttons remain native spells unless a stealth selector is needed; update their ranks manually after training.
