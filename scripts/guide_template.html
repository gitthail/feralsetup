Setup updates macros, bindings, explicit-mouseover settings and fixed bars. Apply both animal pages once. Thereafter A/B switches change only bindings, in any form while out of combat. Personal item slots are preserved; unlearned spells leave empty slots.

Mac / Windows: every Cmd shortcut also works with Alt. On café PCs use Alt. Saved snapshots and preferences live separately in WoW’s WTF folder; installing the addon does not transfer those.

Two layouts. Same action slots.

Focus, targeting and shared utility

Shift+2: focus Kick / Counterspell. Shift+X: focus Blind / Polymorph. Rogue Shift+1: focus Kidney Shot / stealthed Cheap Shot; Shift+T: focus Gouge. Mage Frost Nova is centred on you, so a focus version has no targeting benefit. Shift+Q/E remain damage, not focus. If implemented, explicit mouseover macros would handle eligible targeted spells, with focus and self overrides matching the Druid approach.

Shared proposals: Shift+Space set focus; Cmd/Alt+Shift+Space clear it; Cmd/Alt+X PvP trinket; Cmd/Alt+Z healing potion; Cmd/Alt+B Healthstone; P bandage; [ / ] food / drink; L mana potion where useful; backslash mount; Shift+D damage meter. Every proposed Cmd shortcut has an Alt alternative. Ctrl and wheel bindings remain unassigned for these classes until reviewed; Druid form-wheel bindings must not carry over accidentally.

No charge is being hidden: 2 becomes the actual interrupt on both classes. Rogue mobility talents, additional spec attacks/cooldowns, racials, Sap and Riposte need a final spec review; suggested spare homes are Z for Sap and 6 for Riposte. Mage utility such as Slow Fall, mana gems and conjuring also needs a final utility pass. Empty positions are intentional, not permission to overwrite unrelated personal binds.

Rogue's Slice and Dice needs its own accessible key: a builder/finisher analogy alone misses that maintenance ability. Mage's Q/E pair means filler and conditional damage, not “spam both”; Ice Lance's value depends on frozen targets/procs. The proposed placements are our design judgment, informed by Reholy's Rogue ability priorities and Celz's Frost Mage guide. Their endgame priorities do not establish a levelling rotation.

Explicit mouseover targeting

Heals, cures, buffs, controls, Faerie Fire and Moonfire use macro @mouseover targeting. Native Mouseover Cast is disabled; no hover modifier is required. Ordinary Claw/Rake/Shred attacks still use your selected target.

{{SUPPORT_ROWS}}
KeyAbilityTarget order
Friendly spells: Cmd/Alt forces self. Otherwise living friendly mouseover → living friendly selected target → self. Shift alone is inactive. Controls: Shift forces a living hostile focus; without Shift, living hostile mouseover → living hostile selected target. An invalid focus does not fall back to another enemy. Range, line of sight, immunity and creature-type requirements still apply; these are not tested by help/harm conditions.

A selected mouseover that is out of range remains the intended target: the macro does not silently cast on your selected target instead. Move the cursor away if you want to use the selected target. Moonfire uses hostile hover/target; Rebirth uses dead friendly hover/target.

Leaving form safely

Spell macros no longer execute /cancelform before attempting a cast. Only the explicit H / Leave Form command does. Setup enables autoUnshift=1 out of combat so the client handles normal caster-spell form changes.

Limit: this removes the unconditional macro-driven drop. It does not prove that this client keeps form on every failed cast. Secure macros cannot branch on mana, cooldown, range or line of sight. We do not run combat scripts that change targeting or form based on those conditions. Test a valid hover heal and failed casts safely before relying on the client behaviour in a dangerous pull.

Macro reference

The addon detects learned form numbers and whether Ravage/Pounce are learned when you install/update macros. For manual copying, check these values against your character first. Ordinary attack buttons remain native spells unless a stealth selector is needed; update their ranks manually after training.

Manual previews default to unlearned stealth openers. The installed addon reads your spellbook instead.
