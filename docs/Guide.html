Focus, targeting and shared utility

Shift+2: focus Kick / Counterspell. Shift+X: focus Blind / Polymorph. Rogue Shift+1: focus Kidney Shot / stealthed Cheap Shot; Shift+T: focus Gouge. Mage Frost Nova is centred on you, so a focus version has no targeting benefit. Shift+Q/E remain damage, not focus. Native mouseover handles ordinary eligible targeted spells; explicit focus and applicable self overrides keep the Druid system's approach.

Shared proposals: Shift+Space set focus; Cmd/Alt+Shift+Space clear it; Cmd/Alt+X PvP trinket; Cmd/Alt+Z healing potion; Cmd/Alt+B Healthstone; P bandage; [ / ] food / drink; L mana potion where useful; backslash mount; Shift+D damage meter. Every proposed Cmd shortcut has an Alt alternative. Ctrl and wheel bindings remain unassigned for these classes until reviewed; Druid form-wheel bindings must not carry over accidentally.

No charge is being hidden: 2 becomes the actual interrupt on both classes. Rogue mobility talents, additional spec attacks/cooldowns, racials, Sap and Riposte need a final spec review; suggested spare homes are Z for Sap and 6 for Riposte. Mage utility such as Slow Fall, mana gems and conjuring also needs a final utility pass. Empty positions are intentional, not permission to overwrite unrelated personal binds.

Rogue's Slice and Dice needs its own accessible key: a builder/finisher analogy alone misses that maintenance ability. Mage's Q/E pair means filler and conditional damage, not “spam both”; Ice Lance's value depends on frozen targets/procs. The proposed placements are our design judgment, informed by Reholy's Rogue ability priorities and Celz's Frost Mage guide. Their endgame priorities do not establish a levelling rotation.

Shift+D toggles the native damage meter enabled/disabled. Its visibility follows the game’s meter settings; select Always to view it outside combat. Other Shift/Alt+WASD are unassigned.

Every addon command

CommandWhat it does

/fs guide onEnable automatic level-up reminders again.

Layout commands require being out of combat. Nothing auto-restores at login. Do not save a broken layout over the snapshot you want to recover. The legacy leftkey command remains available, but the current layout does not depend on that keyboard key.

The route, inside the game

Private level-up reminders are enabled by default for Alliance Druids from 15 through 30. They show your recommended current area, next area and training notes on milestone levels.

/fs guide
/fs guide off
/fs guide on

guide shows the current route and quest suggestions whenever you need them. off/on changes automatic reminders for this character. Manual lookup still works while reminders are off.
