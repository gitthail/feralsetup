Use one modifier at a time except the explicitly listed targeting combinations.

Cat Q/Shift+Q = direct builder/finisher; E/Shift+E = bleed builder/finisher.
Bear Q/Shift+Q = single-target damage; E Swipe; Shift+E Lacerate; R Roar.
No macro automatically enters an animal form. To Bash from Cat: wheel down, 1.
Unlearned spells remain unavailable; stealth 1/R have no alternate attack fallback.

MODIFIERS
Shift+1       Focus Pounce in stealth / Bash in Bear / Roots in human.
              Inactive in normal Cat.
Shift+2       Focus Charge in Cat/Bear; focus Hibernate in human.
Shift+6       Focus Growl in Bear.
Alt+Q         Dash in Cat, including stealth. Inactive in Bear/human.
Shift+Q/E are attacks, not focus shortcuts. Alt+1 remains Barkskin.
Native mouseover needs no modifier, including on ordinary attacks.
Global self/focus modifiers are None; macros retain self/focus overrides.
Ctrl is free for user bindings. Z and Shift+R are freed.

UPGRADE TO V2
Restart WoW after replacing files. Out of combat, humanoid form, main page 1:
/fs setup
Enter Cat: /fs form
Enter Bear: /fs form
Then /fs save. All three pages must be applied for the new layout.
Personal item slots are preserved. Spell ranks on native buttons remain manual.
No bar-bug workaround or automatic layout restoration was added.
