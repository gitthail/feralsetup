Use one modifier at a time except the explicitly listed targeting combinations.

MODIFIERS
Native mouseover needs no modifier, including on ordinary attacks.
Global self/focus modifiers are None; macros retain those overrides.
Ctrl is free for user bindings. /fs targeting applies this mode.
Alt+Q         Dash in Cat, including while stealthed. No action in Bear/humanoid.
Shift+Q       Focus Pounce in stealth / focus Bash in Bear; inactive normal Cat.
Shift+E       Focus charge in your current animal form.
Shift+6       Focus Growl in Bear.

MIGRATION
Faerie Fire moved to Shift+R; previous Cmd/Alt+R assignments are removed.
Focus moved from N to Shift+Space; Cmd/Alt+Shift+Space clears it.
Frenzied Regeneration moved from Cmd+R to Cmd/Alt+J.
B remains Challenging Roar in Bear and toggles Prowl only in Cat.
The old N / key-left-of-1 addon assignments are removed if still assigned to
those addon actions. Unrelated replacements on those keys are preserved.
No bar-bug workaround or general spell-rank macro conversion was added.
